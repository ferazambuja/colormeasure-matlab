# Why placement checking is unavailable

The direct PR, CR and CS chart workflows retain acquired radiance/luminance,
instrument-scale XYZ and naming-only chart associations. They do not supply
qualified relative reflective colors, so this installation offers no measured
reference setup or placement-warning decision. A measured mark records an
accepted reading, not a confirmed physical patch identity.

Historical Classic illustrations remain useful for orientation. Their source
white and scale are documented in [chart colors](chart-colors.md). They do not
establish your specimen's edition, backing, geometry or measurement conditions.
Digital SG expected values retain an unestablished source white/observer basis
and receive no inferred reference palette.

A relative reflective comparison would require reference and measured XYZ in
one declared scale, white and observer, matched physical conditions, and a
justified comparison allowance. Radiance cannot become reflectance merely by
normalizing XYZ. The existing [CIELAB calculation](science.md#3-cielab-and-reference-white-meaning) accepts
an explicit reference white; calling it does not establish those prerequisites.
[CIE S 017:2020, term 17-23-076](https://cie.co.at/eilvterm/17-23-076) defines
CIELAB. [Term 17-23-077](https://cie.co.at/eilvterm/17-23-077) defines its
Euclidean color difference, not a physical-position test.

No screen swatch, calibration label or color-difference formula establishes
patch identity or instrument uncertainty. Perceptual difference studies do not
supply universal positioning or sensor-noise limits.
[CIE 230:2019](https://www.cie.co.at/publications/validity-formulae-predicting-small-colour-differences)
studies small adjacent color differences under its experimental conditions.

Guided naming, navigation, selected-reading history, continuation and MAT/CSV
saving remain available without placement checking. Follow the instrument's
preparation guide and keep its measurement area within the intended patch.
No real-equipment validation has been performed for this application.
